# Privacy Policy

This policy explains how [Your company legal name] ("we", "us"), the provider of {app}, handles personal data. It is written to meet India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

## 1. Two kinds of data, two roles

- **Account data** — the details of people who use {app} (your team). We decide why and how this is used, so we are its **Data Fiduciary**.
- **Lead data** — the details of your customers and prospects that you store in {app}. **You** decide why it is collected and used, so you are its Data Fiduciary and we process it only on your instructions, as your **Data Processor**. You are responsible for having a lawful basis (such as consent) to contact your leads.

## 2. What we collect

- **Account details:** name, email, password (stored only as a secure hash), role, workspace name and time zone.
- **Security and usage records:** sign-ins, IP address, browser/device type, and an activity log of changes made in your workspace.
- **Lead data you add or connect:** names, phone numbers, emails, notes, follow-ups and WhatsApp messages, including leads that arrive from your website form, WhatsApp number, or Facebook, Instagram and Google ad forms.
- **Payment details:** when paid plans are enabled, payments are handled by Razorpay. We do not see or store your card or bank details.

## 3. Why we use it

To provide and secure {app}, keep your workspace separate from every other customer's, send you service emails (sign-in, invitations, reminders, trial and billing notices), give support, and meet legal obligations. **We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.**

## 4. Who we share it with

Only service providers we need to run {app}, under contracts that require them to protect it:

- Hosting and database: [hosting provider, region]
- Email delivery: [email provider]
- Meta (WhatsApp Cloud API, Facebook and Instagram lead ads) — only if you connect them
- Google Ads lead forms — only if you connect them
- {ai_provider} (AI assistant): lead details are sent only when a user asks for an analysis, or when a workspace turns on AI follow-through under Autopilot
- Razorpay (payments) — when paid plans are enabled
- Error monitoring: [provider, if enabled]

We may disclose data if required by law.

## 5. How long we keep it

- Your workspace data stays while your account is active.
- The activity log keeps 12 months of history.
- If you delete your workspace, its data is removed from our live systems immediately. Backup copies are overwritten within [30] days.

## 6. How we protect it

Encrypted connections (HTTPS), passwords stored as one-way hashes, integration credentials encrypted at rest, optional two-factor login, strict separation between workspaces, role-based access (agents see only their own leads) and an activity log.

## 7. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, nominate someone to act for you, and raise a grievance. Workspace admins can download all data or delete the workspace from **Settings → Workspace**. If you are a lead whose details a business stored in {app}, please contact that business first; we will help them respond.

## 8. Cookies

We use only essential cookies, to keep you signed in and to protect forms. No advertising or tracking cookies.

{app} is a business tool and is not meant for anyone under 18.
